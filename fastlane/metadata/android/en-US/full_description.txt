GitPockit is a free, open-source Git client for Android.

Keep notes, an Obsidian vault, documents or code on your phone in sync with any Git repository, on GitHub, GitLab, Gitea or your own server.

• Clone over HTTPS or SSH
• Commit, pull, push, or sync everything with one tap
• See changes, history and ahead/behind status
• Premium dark theme, simple light theme and AMOLED black

GitPockit is in early development. Automatic background sync, conflict resolution, branches, stash, rebase and much more are on the way.
